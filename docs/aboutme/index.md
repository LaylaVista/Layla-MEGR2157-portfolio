#About Me
My name is Layla Vista, and I am a sophomore at UNC Charlotte majoring in Mechanical Engineering. Before going to UNC Charlotte, I went to Wake Technical Community College and received an associate in engineering. As a child, I always loved math and building things, so naturally, when it came time to choose a major for college, I fell into mechanical engineering, where I could use my creativity to solve real-world problems. Since I only just recently transferred into mechanical engineering, I am still trying to figure out what industry of mechanical engineering I want to work in. I am interested in industries including manufacturing and product design, and I plan to declare a concentration in Precision Engineering and Metrology in the Spring of 2027. Although I haven’t decided on a specific field yet, I hope that gaining more exposure to the field through this course will help me decide. 
To defend an engineering decision, a person must be able to explain why they chose each aspect of a design, including materials, dimensions, and manufacturing processes. The person must be able to explain the constraints of the design, such as cost or weight of the final product, and be able to explain the potential tradeoffs of the decision they made. Since this is only my first year as a mechanical engineering student, I am not confident in my own ability to defend an engineering decision; however, that is something I want to improve throughout this course.
